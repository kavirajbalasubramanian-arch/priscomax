How we protect your PHI as a US vendor based in India.

US buyers should ask hard questions before sharing patient data. This page explains our approach in plain terms. Anything you cannot verify here, ask us for in writing.

The BAA comes first

We sign a Business Associate Agreement before any PHI is handled, including for a sample batch or pilot. You can send your own BAA or use ours. The agreement sets out permitted uses, safeguards, breach reporting and what happens to the data when the work ends.

Offshore delivery, US obligations

HIPAA applies to a Business Associate directly, wherever it is located. Our BAA, access controls and incident procedures are written with that in mind. Some payer contracts and state programs restrict offshore access to PHI, so we review your contracts with you before any work starts.

Before we begin

  • BAA signed by both parties
  • Offshore access reviewed against your payer contracts
  • Access plan agreed: who sees what, in which system
  • Incident contacts and breach-notice timelines agreed
  • Return or destruction of data defined for the end of the work

Administrative, physical and technical controls.

Administrative

  • Regular risk review of where PHI lives and moves
  • Written policies, HIPAA training and confidentiality agreements
  • BAAs with any subcontractor, to the same standard
  • Report, contain and document incidents under breach notification rules
  • Backup and recovery plans for outages

Physical

  • Delivery center limited to authorized staff, with biometric entry
  • Clear-desk policy: no paper, phones or personal storage at workstations
  • External printing and removable or recording media disabled on work systems
  • PHI returned or destroyed as your agreement states, with a record

Technical

  • Unique logins and role-based access
  • Multi-factor sign-in to client systems
  • Encryption in transit and at rest, with secure VPN into client EHRs
  • Endpoint and access activity logged and reviewed
  • Automatic logoff on idle sessions

Safeguards are confirmed in each client agreement. Ask for the current version during due diligence.

How PHI moves through our process.

  1. 1. ReceiveThrough a secure channel you approve
  2. 2. Limit accessOnly the staff who need it
  3. 3. Code and reviewWith a second-reviewer quality check
  4. 4. ReturnEncrypted, logged and confirmed
  5. 5. Retain or disposeAs your agreement states

What to ask any offshore coding vendor.

Use this list with us or with anyone else. A good vendor answers each question directly and in writing.

Read the BAA page
  • Will you sign our BAA before we send a sample?
  • Where do your staff work from, and who can enter that facility?
  • Do coders work in our systems, or is data copied to yours?
  • How do you handle a suspected breach, and how fast do you tell us?
  • Which of our payer contracts restrict offshore access?
  • How is access removed when someone leaves the project?
  • Which security or compliance certifications do you hold today, and which are in progress?
