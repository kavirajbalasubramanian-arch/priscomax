Discuss your project
Do you sign a Business Associate Agreement?

Yes. We sign a BAA before any PHI is handled, including for a sample batch. You can send your own or use ours.

You are based in India. Is that allowed for our PHI?

HIPAA applies to us directly as a Business Associate, wherever we are located, and the BAA binds us to it. Some payer contracts and state programs restrict offshore access to PHI, so we check yours with you before any work starts.

Where does our PHI stay?

Our coders work inside your systems over a secure connection where possible. Printing and removable media are disabled on our work systems.

Who does the coding and quality checks?

CPC and CCS certified coders do the coding. An independent quality reviewer checks the work before submission, and error trends go back to the coders weekly.

What does it cost after the pilot?

Pricing is agreed in your contract after the pilot, based on specialty, volume and scope. We do not ask for a long-term commitment to start.

Will your team work in our time zone?

We arrange overlap with US working hours for questions and escalations, and agree the schedule with you during onboarding.

Is this website a secure channel for PHI?

No. Please do not send patient information through the contact form or by email until a BAA is in place.
